# ProctorGuard — Online Exam Instructions

**Please read this document fully before starting your exam.**
ProctorGuard is an AI-assisted online proctoring system. It uses your camera, microphone, and screen to make sure the exam is fair for everyone. The system is designed to allow **normal human behaviour** — thinking, reading, glancing at your screen — and only flags genuinely suspicious activity.

---

## 1. Before You Begin — Requirements

### Equipment
- A **laptop or desktop** computer (mobile phones/tablets are not supported for taking the exam).
- A **working webcam** facing you, at eye level if possible.
- A **working microphone**.
- A **stable internet connection**.
- An up-to-date browser — **Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge** is strongly recommended.

### Environment
- Sit in a **quiet, well-lit room**, alone.
- Make sure **light falls on your face** (don't sit with a bright window behind you).
- Clear your desk of books, papers, notes, phones, and any second device.
- **Disconnect any external/second monitors** before starting.
- Close all other apps, tabs, and chat/notification software.

---

## 2. How to Attend the Exam — Step by Step

1. **Log in** to the exam portal with the credentials provided to you.
2. **Select your exam** from the list when it becomes available.
3. **Grant permissions** when the browser asks:
   - **Camera** — to confirm you are present. **Required** — the exam will not start without it.
   - **Microphone** — to detect background voices. **Required** — the exam will not start without it.
   - **Screen share** — you must choose **"Entire Screen"** in the picker (not a Window or a Chrome Tab). **Required** — if you pick anything other than your entire screen, the system will reject it and ask you to re-share correctly. This is recorded for review.
   - **Location** — used only to confirm you haven't moved somewhere else mid-exam. This is **optional**: if you deny it, you can still start and complete the exam normally.
   > The exam will **not start** until Camera, Microphone, and Screen Share (entire screen) are granted. Location is the only permission that will never block you from starting.
4. **Identity enrollment (face capture):**
   - Look straight at the camera in good lighting, with your **full face visible**.
   - Remove hats, masks, and sunglasses.
   - Click **"Capture & Verify Identity."** This becomes your reference photo for the whole exam.
5. **Enter fullscreen** when prompted. The exam must stay in fullscreen the entire time.
6. The **exam begins.** Read each question carefully. Use the navigation buttons to move between questions/sections.
7. Watch the **timer** (top of the screen). Some exams also have **per-section time limits** — a section may lock once you move on.
8. Your progress is **auto-saved** every few seconds. If your connection drops, you can usually **resume** where you left off.
9. When finished, click **Submit**. If the timer runs out, the exam **submits automatically**.
10. After submitting, you may be asked to **rate the exam and leave feedback**.

## 3. Violations the System Detects

The system continuously watches for the following. Brief, momentary events are ignored — only **sustained or clear** behaviour is recorded. Each recorded violation may include a **snapshot** for the proctor to review.

| Violation | What triggers it | When it fires |
|---|---|---|
| **NO_FACE** | Your face is not visible to the camera | After **~10 seconds** of no face |
| **MULTIPLE_FACES** | More than one person in the camera view | After **~2.5 seconds** of a second person |
| **GAZE_AWAY** | Head turned far away from the screen | After **~9 seconds** of a hard, sustained turn |
| **PHONE_DETECTED** | A mobile phone appears in view | On a confident, repeated detection |
| **ANOMALY_OBJECT** | A book, notes, second laptop, or extra screen in view | On a confident, repeated detection |
| **AUDIO_DETECTED** | Sustained talking / background conversation | After a short calibration + several seconds of speech |
| **TAB_SWITCH** | Switching tabs, minimising, or leaving the exam window | Immediately |
| **FULLSCREEN_EXIT** | Leaving fullscreen mode | Immediately |
| **COPY_PASTE** | Copy, cut, paste, print, save, or screenshot attempts | Immediately (the action is also blocked) |
| **IDENTITY_CHANGE** | The person on camera no longer matches your enrolled face | On a sustained mismatch |
| **LOCATION_CHANGE** | Significant change in your physical location during the exam | On a confirmed movement |

> **Severity:** Violations are graded **High / Medium / Low**. A brief look away (Low) is treated very differently from a phone or a second person (High). Proctors review the context — not every flag means cheating.

### Violation limits & termination
Some exams set a **maximum number of violations** for certain categories (for example, tab switches or copy/paste). If you reach that limit, the exam may be **automatically terminated**. You will see a clear on-screen warning before this happens where possible.

## 4. Do's ✅

- **Do** keep your **face centred** and visible in the camera at all times.
- **Do** stay in **fullscreen** for the entire exam.
- **Do** sit in a **quiet, private, well-lit** room, alone.
- **Do** keep your desk **clear** of phones, books, notes, and extra devices.
- **Do** look at your **screen** — reading and thinking are perfectly normal.
- **Do** ensure a **stable internet** connection before starting.
- **Do** close all **other applications and browser tabs** beforehand.
- **Do** read the **warning banners** that appear at the top of the screen and correct your behaviour.

## 5. Don'ts ❌

- **Don't** leave the exam window, switch tabs, or minimise the browser.
- **Don't** exit fullscreen.
- **Don't** let **anyone else** appear on camera or talk to you.
- **Don't** use a **phone, second screen, or any other device**.
- **Don't** keep **books, notes, or papers** within camera view.
- **Don't** try to **copy, paste, print, screenshot, or open developer tools** — these are blocked and logged.
- **Don't** cover your face, wear sunglasses/hats, or leave the frame for long.
- **Don't** speak out loud or have conversations in the room.
- **Don't** disconnect your camera, microphone, or screen share during the exam.

## 6. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Camera/mic permission denied | Click the camera icon in the browser address bar → **Allow**, then retry. |
| "Wrong Screen Shared" appears | You picked a Window or Chrome Tab in the share dialog. Click **Try Again**, choose the **"Entire Screen"** tab in the picker, select your monitor, then **Share**. |
| "Permissions Required" keeps showing | Make sure you shared your **entire screen**, not just a window/tab. |
| Location permission denied | No action needed — location is optional and does not block your exam. |
| I'm on the same Wi-Fi/network as other candidates | This is fine. Attending from the same public IP address as other candidates (shared lab, office, or campus network) is **not** treated as a violation. |
| Face not detected at enrollment | Face the camera directly, improve lighting, remove glasses/hat, move closer. |
| "Disconnect External Displays" | Unplug any second monitor and click **Recheck**. |
| Connection dropped | Reconnect — your answers are auto-saved and you can usually **Resume**. |
| Fullscreen exited accidentally | Click **Enter Fullscreen** to continue. |

---

## 7. Privacy

Your camera, microphone, and screen are recorded **only during the exam** for integrity review by authorised proctors. Your face signature is stored securely and used **only** to confirm it is you during the exam. Recordings and violation snapshots are retained per your institution's policy.

---

*If you experience a genuine technical issue during the exam, note the time and contact your exam administrator immediately.*
